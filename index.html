Research

My research concerns the mathematical modeling, analysis, and computation of interfacial fluid flows, with applications ranging from pulmonary mechanics to coating flows and dynamic wetting. I am particularly interested in the interaction of capillarity, surfactants, rheology, and hydrodynamic instabilities.

Current research

Odd viscosity and thin-film instabilities

My current research examines the influence of odd viscosity on thin-film stability and dynamics, including gravitational and thermocapillary effects.

Related background: thin-film instability publications

Current research

Multilayer films in cylindrical tubes

With Awa Traore, I study the stability and nonlinear dynamics of multilayer viscous flows in cylindrical tubes.

Preprint: Stability and nonlinear dynamics of three-layer viscous films inside a vertical cylindrical tube

Current and recent research

Dynamic wetting, contact lines, and fluid rheology

My research on moving contact lines and droplet spreading addresses the effects of fluid rheology and surfactants. Recent work with Hsien-Hung Wei concerns dynamic wetting in power-law fluids.

Representative paper: Moving contact lines of power-law fluids (JFM, 2026)

Ongoing and established research

Pulmonary liquid plugs and airway mechanics

A longstanding research interest is the mechanics of liquid films, bubbles, and plugs in pulmonary airways. I continue to develop mathematical models of liquid-plug propagation and surfactant transport.

Representative paper: Reduced-dimension model of liquid plug propagation in tubes (2016)

Established research

Surfactant-driven flows and interfacial stability

Earlier and continuing investigations address transport of soluble and insoluble surfactants on liquid films, Marangoni-driven spreading, and surfactant-induced instability in shear flows. A recurring goal is to understand how surface-tension gradients couple to flow, transport, and interface deformation, and how these effects can either suppress or enhance instability.

Earlier research descriptions: surfactant spreading · thin-film instabilities
